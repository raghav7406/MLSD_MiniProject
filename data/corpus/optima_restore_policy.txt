Optima Restore Health Insurance Policy
Policy Document – Version 1.8
Effective Date: 1 July 2024
Insurer: Star Health and Allied Insurance Co. Ltd.

== Coverage Summary ==

This policy provides coverage for in-patient hospitalisation, day-care procedures,
pre and post hospitalisation expenses, and emergency ambulance charges.

== In-Patient Hospitalisation ==

Eligible planned hospitalisation is payable up to the available sum insured.
The insurer may request medical records when diagnosis or procedure details
are incomplete.

Coverage includes room rent, surgeon fees, anaesthesia charges, medicines,
diagnostic tests, ICU charges, and nursing expenses as per policy terms.

== Cataract Surgery ==

Cataract surgery is covered after 24 months of continuous policy tenure under
Code-Excl02. Treatment before completion of the waiting period is excluded.

The sub-limit for cataract surgery is INR 40,000 per eye under this policy.
This includes the cost of intraocular lens (IOL), surgical charges, and
related pre and post operative expenses.

== Waiting Periods ==

Initial waiting period of 30 days applies to all illnesses except accidental
injuries.

Specific disease waiting period of 24 months (Code-Excl02) applies to:
cataract, hernia, joint replacement, piles, sinusitis, tonsillectomy,
kidney stones, hydrocele, and other listed conditions.

Pre-existing disease waiting period of 48 months (Code-Excl01) applies to
all conditions existing before policy inception.

== Exclusions ==

Permanent exclusions include cosmetic surgery, dental treatment (non-accidental),
obesity surgery, self-inflicted injuries, substance abuse treatment, and
experimental procedures.

== Room Rent ==

Room rent eligibility is based on the elected room category:
- General Ward: actual charges
- Semi-Private: up to 1% of sum insured per day
- Private: up to 2% of sum insured per day

Proportionate deduction applies if a higher room category is used.

== Restore Benefit ==

If the sum insured is exhausted during the policy year, 100% of the base
sum insured is automatically restored for claims arising from a different
illness or injury.

== Pre-Authorisation ==

All planned hospitalisations require pre-authorisation. The insurer will
respond within 24 hours for planned cases and 2 hours for emergencies.
Emergency hospitalisation is covered subject to intimation within 24 hours.
