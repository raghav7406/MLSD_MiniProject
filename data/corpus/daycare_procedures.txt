Day-Care Procedures List
Care Supreme Health Insurance Policy – Appendix A
Effective Date: 1 April 2024

The following procedures are covered as day-care treatments under the policy.
Day-care treatment means medically necessary treatment that requires less than
24 hours of hospitalisation due to advances in medical technology.

1. Cataract surgery (phacoemulsification with IOL implant)
2. Dialysis (haemodialysis and peritoneal dialysis)
3. Chemotherapy (all cycles per policy year)
4. Radiotherapy (all sessions per policy year)
5. Lithotripsy (ESWL for kidney and ureteric stones)
6. Tonsillectomy
7. Adenoidectomy
8. Sinusitis surgery (FESS - Functional Endoscopic Sinus Surgery)
9. Dental surgery arising from accidental injury only
10. Coronary angiography (diagnostic)
11. Coronary angioplasty (PTCA with or without stent)
12. Hysteroscopy (diagnostic and therapeutic)
13. Dilation and Curettage (D&C)
14. Fracture management including plastering and closed reduction
15. Hydrocele surgery
16. Arthroscopy (diagnostic and therapeutic)
17. Colonoscopy (therapeutic with polypectomy)
18. Upper GI endoscopy (therapeutic)
19. Laparoscopic cholecystectomy (gall bladder removal)
20. Laparoscopic appendectomy
21. Hernia repair (inguinal, umbilical, femoral – laparoscopic)
22. Piles, fissure, and fistula surgery (stapler haemorrhoidectomy)
23. Varicocele surgery
24. Circumcision (medically necessary)
25. Skin lesion excision and biopsy
26. Breast lump excision (fibroadenoma)
27. Ganglion cyst excision
28. Carpal tunnel release surgery
29. Trigger finger release
30. Tympanoplasty (ear drum repair)
31. Myringotomy with grommet insertion
32. Nasal polypectomy
33. Septoplasty (deviated nasal septum correction)
34. Pterygium excision (eye)
35. Chalazion excision (eye)
36. Incision and drainage of abscess
37. Urethral dilatation
38. Cystoscopy (diagnostic and therapeutic)
39. TURP (Transurethral Resection of Prostate) – if as day-care
40. Injection of sclerosant for varicose veins

Note: Coverage for each day-care procedure is subject to:
- The terms and conditions of the policy
- Applicable waiting periods (see Section 5 of the policy)
- Applicable sub-limits (see Section 7 of the policy)
- The procedure being medically necessary as certified by the treating doctor
- The available sum insured at the time of treatment

Procedures not listed above may be covered on a case-by-case basis if they
meet the definition of day-care treatment under IRDAI guidelines.
